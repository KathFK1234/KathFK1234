# 2026-10 — continuing MindConnect on my own, as Healing Hive

MindConnect was a team project in 2025. Four of us built the skeleton between June and September.

I came back to it in October 2026 meaning to make a few changes. A few changes in, it was clear I was really starting a second version, so I gave it its own repository and its own name.

What I kept: the purpose, the features, the tagline, and the product thinking about where a person decides whether to keep going.

What I changed first was not the screens. It was the parts nobody sees until they fail: who is allowed to be called a professional, whether two people can book the same hour, and what happens when someone types something frightening and the AI is not answering.

Lesson: a first version proves the idea can exist. A second version is where you decide what it must never get wrong.
