# The loop — observe, hypothesise, test, learn, iterate

Psychology, backend engineering, AI, and education are not separate careers for me. They are one habit of mind:

  1. observe what actually happens
  2. form a hypothesis
  3. build a small test
  4. learn from reality
  5. iterate

Whether the subject is a student, a user flow, an API, or a model output, the loop is the same.
