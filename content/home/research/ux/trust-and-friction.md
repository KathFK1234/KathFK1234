# Trust and friction — what people do when tired, confused, or rushed

I think about motivation, friction, trust, and context: what people actually do when they are tired, confused, or under time pressure.

The question is never only "can this work technically?" It is also "why would someone keep using it, trust it, and understand it?"

Things I watch for:

- where a person hesitates
- where the product asks for something before it has earned it
- where the system knows something the person does not, and stays quiet
