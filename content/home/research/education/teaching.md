# Teaching — what leading a classroom teaches about building systems

I am a School Lead Educator at TechLit Africa, leading hands-on digital literacy learning and mentoring students through creativity, problem-solving, and self-expression with technology. Before that I volunteered as an educator at Joy Village.

A classroom is a fast feedback loop. If an explanation does not land, you know within a minute, and the fix is never "the student should have understood".

The same holds for software: when people struggle with a system, the system is the thing to change.
