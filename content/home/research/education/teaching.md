# Teaching — what leading a classroom teaches about building systems

I am a School Lead Educator at TechLit Africa, leading daily digital literacy sessions at Murengeti Primary School: some curriculum progress, some focused practice, some open exploration. Before that I volunteered as an educator at Joy-Village, teaching psychological skills to teenagers.

A classroom is a fast feedback loop. If an explanation does not land, you know within a minute, and the fix is never "the student should have understood".

Typing practice was the proof. Some learners had been stuck on level one for a long time. The learners were fine; the practice was not. Once I redesigned it, they started moving steadily through the levels.

The same holds for software: when people struggle with a system, the system is the thing to change.
