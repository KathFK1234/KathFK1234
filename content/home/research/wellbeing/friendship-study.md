# Personality and friendship selection — undergraduate research study

A behavioral research study on personality traits and friendship selection, carried out with a five-person team at Kenyatta University.

  my role        coordinated survey collection, led the analysis
  respondents    150+
  method         survey-based

What it taught me about building software: the data you collect is shaped by how you ask, and the answer you report is shaped by what you chose to count.
