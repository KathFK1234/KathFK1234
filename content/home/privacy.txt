This site has no cookies, no analytics and no accounts.

Your theme, font and command history stay in your own browser. `history -c` clears the history.
Whatever you type after `mood` is sent to MoodForecast AI to answer you: places, plans such as a picnic,
and the first letters of a place it cannot find, to suggest one. So type places and plans there, not anything
about yourself. MoodForecast AI's own privacy page says what it keeps: https://moodforecastai-production.up.railway.app/privacy.html
Nothing else you type leaves this page.
The hosts log requests too: GitHub Pages stores each visitor's IP address for security, and Railway's request logs record it.
