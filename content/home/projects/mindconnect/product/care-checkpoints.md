# Care checkpoints — places where trust and clarity matter most

A checkpoint is a moment where a person decides whether to keep going.

  first screen       Is this for someone like me?
  first disclosure   What happens to what I just typed?
  the wait           Did anyone receive it? How long will this take?
  the handoff        Who am I talking to now, and why?
  leaving            Can I stop without it counting against me?

Design rule: at every checkpoint, answer the question before it is asked.
