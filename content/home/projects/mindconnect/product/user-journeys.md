# User journeys — low-friction support flows for young people

The journeys are written from the person's state, not from the feature list.

1. "I am not sure anything is wrong."
   Needs: a low-stakes way in. No account wall, no clinical language.

2. "Something is wrong and I do not know who to tell."
   Needs: a clear, short path to a human or a resource. Every extra screen is a place to give up.

3. "I have asked for help before and it went badly."
   Needs: evidence that this time is different. Say what happens next, who sees what, and how to leave.

In each journey the measure is the same: does the next step feel clear?
