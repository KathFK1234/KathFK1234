# Research — the questions underneath the design

MindConnect starts from a psychology question rather than a feature: what makes a young person take the next step toward support, and what makes them stop?

Working questions:

- Which words make support feel accessible, and which make it feel clinical?
- What does a young person need to know before disclosing anything?
- Where does anonymity help, and where does it get in the way of care?
- What should the system do when a person goes quiet halfway through?
