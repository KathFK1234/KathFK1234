# MindConnect — a mental-health support concept for young people in Kenya

A platform concept designed around the realities of young people in Kenya: accessibility, empathy, and practical support. The aim is a digital experience where the next step always feels clear.

This is concept work, built with a team.

  stack: Figma · MongoDB · Express · Node.js · AI
