# Concept notes — early interaction ideas and product questions

- Lead with one question, not a form.
- Let a person browse resources before the product asks who they are.
- Keep emotional check-ins short enough to finish when tired.
- Make the route to a human visible from every screen.
- Treat "not now" as a valid answer, and make it easy to come back.

Screens were explored in Figma before any backend work.
